PRIVACY POLICY.

Effective date: August 8, 2026

1. Overview

White Line Transport L.L.C. respects the privacy of visitors to this website. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected through the website, how that information may be used, and the choices available to visitors.

2. Information We May Collect

When you contact White Line Transport through the website, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, company name, email address, telephone number, and freight or service details. Technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, and basic website usage information may also be processed by hosting, security, or analytics services if those services are enabled.

3. How Information Is Used

  • Responding to quote requests, contracting inquiries, and other messages.
  • Communicating about transportation services and business inquiries.
  • Evaluating and coordinating freight transportation requests.
  • Maintaining website security, functionality, and reliability.
  • Complying with applicable legal, regulatory, or contractual requirements.

4. Sharing of Information

White Line Transport does not sell personal information as a business practice. Information may be shared with service providers, technology providers, communications providers, transportation partners, professional advisers, government authorities, or other parties when reasonably necessary to provide requested services, operate the website, protect rights and safety, or comply with law.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies when necessary for basic functionality, security, analytics, or other website services. If third-party analytics or embedded services are enabled, those providers may process technical or usage information according to their own privacy practices.

6. Data Security

White Line Transport uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide services, maintain business and compliance records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or satisfy legal and regulatory obligations.

8. Third-Party Websites and Services

This website may contain links to third-party websites or services. White Line Transport is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party sites. Visitors should review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing information.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business and general audiences and is not directed toward children under 13. White Line Transport does not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13.

10. Your Privacy Requests

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or ask questions about how information is handled. Requests should include enough information for us to identify the relevant inquiry while avoiding unnecessary sensitive information.
