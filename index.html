ABOUT WHITE LINE TRANSPORT

MOVING FREIGHT.
DELIVERING TRUST.

White Line Transport L.L.C. is a Harrisonburg, Virginia-based small business focused on dependable freight transportation, dispatching, and fleet operations.

Our transportation operations are built around reliable execution, professional communication, safety awareness, and responsible fleet management.

As a long-distance general freight truckload carrier, White Line Transport is positioned to support customers, brokers, transportation partners, and contracting organizations requiring dependable freight movement.
